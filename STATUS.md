**Three limits, kept apart** — reference agreement is *not* a statement about what is drawn:

| Limit | Size | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Solar position vs astronomy-engine | ≤ **0.0124°** | the arithmetic only |
| Texture quantisation at 1024×512 | ~**0.35°** | one pixel of the canvas |
| Staleness between one-minute repaints | up to **0.25°** | Earth's rotation between recomputes |

These are three different kinds of statement and are **not summed into a single bound** — doing so would assert a measured figure that was never measured. Separately: the solar position **agrees with the reference to within 0.0124°** over the stated range; the shading is **quantised to ~0.35° by the 1024×512 texture**; and between repaints the line **drifts by up to 0.25° of rotation**. Separately again, the geometric boundary is about **0.83°** from an observed sunrise, for the refraction and solar-disc reasons above. The only one of these that is a *measurement* is the first.
