Recorded animal journeys

A journey here is a set of positions recorded by a tag on one animal, replayed against the dates they were recorded. It is a dated record, not a live position. The marker is always on a recorded fix. The line joins recorded fixes; the path between fixes was not recorded.

An archive feature. Every other layer on Mother Earth shows live or regularly updated data. These journeys are finished studies from past years, shown as dated records. If regularly updated tracking is added later it will carry its own dates and freshness labels.

The two sea journeys are experimental. Their positions are Argos satellite fixes, far less exact than GPS. Mother Earth has left out fixes under rules of its own, listed on each card. They are display choices, not a reconstruction of where the animal went. A gap in a sea journey means no fix is displayed there; the source may hold fixes that were left out. On the globe their fix marks are drawn large so they can be seen; the size of a mark does not show uncertainty.
