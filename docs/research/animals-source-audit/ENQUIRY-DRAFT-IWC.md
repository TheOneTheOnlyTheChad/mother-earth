## Draft

> Subject: Permission to display an IWC population status summary (Southern Hemisphere humpback, Western Indian Ocean) on an educational website
>
> Dear IWC Secretariat
>
> I run Mother Earth, a public website presenting observations of the planet with their sources, dates and limitations (https://theonetheonlythechad.github.io/mother-earth/). I am preparing an Animals page and would like one card on it to show the IWC's assessment of a recovered whale population, in the IWC's own terms.
>
> Your copyright page asks for written permission before material from iwc.int is republished, so I am asking before anything is built.
>
> 1. Permission. From the "Status of Whale Populations" table, for "Humpback: Southern Hemisphere, Western Indian Ocean (BSC)", I would like to display:
>    - the status sentence ("Abundant and recovered after strong growth.");
>    - the abundance figure and its range (18,111; 15,580-20,700);
>    - the Relative Abundance class (Very Good);
>    - the 20-year change class and its period (Growth 1995-2015, Strongly Increased);
>    - a link to the IWC page and the acknowledgement you require.
>
> 2. Three things I could not establish from the pages, and do not want to guess:
>    - which year the abundance figure refers to, and how it was derived (the model or paper behind it);
>    - what the range is (for example a 95% confidence or credible interval);
>    - how you would like the dates presented. The status summary gives the assessment date as 2015, and the table is marked "last update: 2026". I would show these as two different things unless you advise otherwise.
>
> 3. Conditions. What citation, acknowledgement and display guidelines apply? Are there conditions on scope or duration?
>
> How it would be shown: the text and numbers above would appear on a public web page, delivered to visitors' browsers, with the IWC named as the source and the assessment date beside them. I would not copy the IWC's graphics. I would keep this population separate from any other stock and from any other species on the page. If any of the wording or figures above is out of date or wrong, I would be grateful for the correction.
>
> About the project: [CHAD: one sentence, from verified facts, on how the site is funded and its intended use.] If your permission depends on non-commercial use or any other limit, please say so and I will record it as a condition.
>
> If this should go to someone else, I would appreciate the right contact.
>
> Ta
> Chad
