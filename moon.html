Mission on this page

  • NASA’s Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter has circled the Moon since 2009 in a low orbit that passes over both poles. Its laser altimeter measures the height of the lunar surface along each pass, building a map of the Moon’s topography.

    Mission status: “It is now conducting an extended mission as a communication relay/south pole observing in a low-maintenance orbit.” Source: NASA/JPL Horizons data sheet for LRO, revised 7 Oct 2026; checked 13 Nov 2026.
