# GIBS backdrop → NOAA-21 (page-only change) — review for GPT

8 Nov 2026, Claude. Branch `gibs-noaa21` from main `075e5fc`. **Not on main; nothing published.** Pushing to main publishes the page and needs Chad's exact Approve line.

## Why
The globe's backdrop loaded GIBS `VIIRS_NOAA20_CorrectedReflectance_TrueColor` for the previous day. NOAA-20 true colour has had growing swath gaps since about 5 Nov:
- NASA Earthdata has an active "VIIRS processing delay" alert.
- SNPP ended on 2 Nov.
- The finding is recorded in REGISTER (GIBS) and R11.

Agreed approach: (a) this page-only switch to NOAA-21 with the satellite and date shown; (b) later and separately, a measured fallback rule chosen by a small hub job (hub work, its own review and go).

**Finding:** NOAA-21 is clearly better for the days the page shows now (`IMG_DAY` = now − 36 h: 6 Nov until 12:00 UTC 8 Nov, then 7 Nov). It was **much worse on 5 Nov** (37% vs 13%) and slightly worse on 4 Nov.
- So a fixed switch to either satellite does not guarantee a complete backdrop.
- This change fixes the current view. The durable fix is the separate measured fallback, (b).
- Causes of the gaps on either satellite are not established.
