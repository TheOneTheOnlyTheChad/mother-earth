- **Night lights (optional layer and two-night comparison), added <date of filing>:** two more layers from the same service, under the same terms and the same single LANCE acknowledgment already in the footer.
  - **`VIIRS_NOAA20_DayNightBand_At_Sensor_Radiance`** — light measured at night, nW/(cm² sr), drawn on NASA's fixed grey scale (colour key `colormaps/v1.3/VIIRS_DayNightBand_At_Sensor_Radiance.xml`: 180 values from 0 in steps of 0.1 at the dark end, everything at or above 38.2 white). Read 15 Nov 2026: daily, 2024-03-25 to 2026-11-15; no data arrives as transparency.
  - **`VIIRS_NOAA20_Clear_Sky_Confidence_Night`** — NASA's clear-sky confidence, 0 to 1 in hundredths (colour key `colormaps/v1.3/VIIRS_Clear_Sky_Confidence.xml`, 101 values). Read 15 Nov 2026: daily to 2026-11-15, with a gap in late 2023 before the light layer begins.
  - **Access:** WMS 1.3.0, `CRS=EPSG:4326`, `BBOX` south,west,north,east (the capabilities give the layer's box latitude first), PNG, from the reader's browser only: nothing when the page opens. On switching the layer on: the light key once, then for each date tried one 512 × 256 frame and, only where that frame passes the rule, one 2048 × 1024 frame. **Maximum by the code:** four dates, so at most four 512 × 256 frames and four 2048 × 1024 frames for one switch-on. **Observed (15 Nov 2026):** one or two 512 × 256 frames and one 2048 × 1024 frame (0.89–0.92 MB read). For one comparison: the two keys (each asked for once per page load while it loads) and four pictures of at most 480 × 480. No hub request, nothing stored. Responses carry `access-control-allow-origin: *`.
  - **Dates (read 15 Nov 2026 18:10 UTC):** the capabilities give `nearestValue="0"` for the light layer; five dates outside its range each returned the same empty 302-byte picture, as did three dates outside the clear-sky layer's ranges. GIBS did not substitute another date in any request made. Nothing in the response headers names the date served.
  - **Our rules:** the globe shows the newest UTC date whose displayed 2048 × 1024 frame has no more than 1% no data between 30°S and 30°N (our rule, not NASA's; a check that a frame has arrived, not a coverage claim). In the comparison a pixel is compared only where light was measured on both nights and clear-sky confidence is 0.95 or more on both (our threshold, not a NASA standard). A request not answered within 20 seconds counts as not answered.
  - **What the page says:** light measured at the satellite, uncorrected for moonlight, cloud, fires, lightning, boats or viewing angle; never a statement about power. The LANCE local-scale line is shown with the layer and with every comparison.
  - **Published guidance on volume (GIBS API docs, "Bulk Downloading", read 15 Nov 2026 through a summarising fetch; GPT read it independently):** a bulk download is a planned retrieval of more than 1,000,000 imagery tiles in 24 hours, to be arranged with Earthdata support at least 48 hours ahead. The page states no limit for interactive map requests. This use is interactive: requests are made only when a reader switches the layer on or presses Compare. The site's total traffic to GIBS was not measured. The guidance is recorded as that, not as a permission or a limit.

  - **Seasonal gap (read 15 Nov 2026):** on 21 June 2026 the light layer had no data north of 60°N and none for about a quarter of 30–60°N; in mid-November almost none south of 60°S.
  - **Not used:** the gap-filled layer, NOAA-21, Suomi NPP (ended 2 Nov 2026), the LAADS data files.
