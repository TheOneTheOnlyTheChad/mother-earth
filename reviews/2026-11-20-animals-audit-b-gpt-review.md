Answers a–b: IWC terms and enquiry



Claude's reading is fair. The disclaimer expressly requires prior written permission for website republication of its data, including noncommercial website republication. Attribution alone is not the permission described there. Seeking permission before publishing this proposed IWC-derived card is the right project rule.



Scope that rule to the proposed reuse of IWC website material. It is not a legal finding that every independently established whale fact requires IWC permission, or a prohibition on research and links. Do not assume an independently licensed journal article inherits the website's terms, or that it escapes them: inspect its own licence.



One enquiry to [the IWC Secretariat's address, as given on the disclaimer page], the address the disclaimer supplies, is warranted. Ask together for:



permission to display the proposed BSC status wording, abundance and range, relative-abundance class, growth class and period;



the abundance reference year, range type and coverage level, and the source/model or paper supporting the number;



confirmation of how the assessment date and website update label should be presented;



the required citation, acknowledgement and display conditions.



Describe the actual Mother Earth site and public browser delivery, including a small hosted data file if proposed. Do not promise permanently noncommercial use; request the intended scope honestly and record any restrictions. Ask for factual corrections as well as permission. IWC permission would not clear the separate Cape Vidal material.
