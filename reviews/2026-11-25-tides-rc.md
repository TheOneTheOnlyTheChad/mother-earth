## 3. One change from the proposal

**Display constants tuned once in the first preview and frozen: base 1.15 (was 1.12), factor 0.12 (was 0.08).** At 0.08 the 3-D surface's change between spring and neap was about 9 screen pixels at the desktop size and hard to see. The surface now runs from 1.062 to 1.325 Earth radii, still never touching the globe; one base and one factor for every angle and mode (tested, T4c, C2). A bright line traces the surface's edge in the orbit plane, the same line as the cross-section.

Also added in the build: one line under the scene, "The Sun's grey half is outside its picture's view, not shadow", because in the Angled view the plain half reads like a night side.
