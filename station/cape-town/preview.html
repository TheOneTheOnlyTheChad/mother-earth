How entries are sorted. The receiver's software (readsb) gives each entry a message type and, when the sender transmits one, an ADS-B emitter category. Categories C1 and C2 are surface vehicles and C3 to C5 are fixed obstacles: those are not aircraft. Categories A1 to A7 and B1 to B7 are things that fly (aeroplanes, helicopters, gliders, balloons, drones and the like) and are counted with the aircraft, whether in the air or on the ground. Type adsb_icao_nt is an ADS-B sender that is not a transponder, which readsb documents with the example of a ground vehicle; with no usable category it could be a vehicle or an aircraft, so it is listed above as kind not stated and kept out of the aircraft count and the plot. Every other entry without a usable category is counted as an aircraft on its message type alone, and the line above says how many.
